# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (first release, alpha)

- New career as a historical officer: a setup window replaces the questionnaire (personality, prestige, money, share of recorded experience; or skip for defaults). You pick the officer when the campaign opens, in the officer window or a searchable list.
- Garrison commanders and officers without a command can be picked; they choose a field command from the start list and keep their rank.
- Mid-career take-over of a historical officer (Ctrl+Shift+H), and an optional listing of officers who had left before the scenario start (Ctrl+Shift+G).
- After the switch, units under your command, running engagements (the **Play manually** option), the hall of fame, and the career button and window portraits are refreshed. The hall of fame no longer lists the historical officer twice.
- In every career, the engagement window rechecks whether you have units in the battle, fixing two vanilla cases where **Play manually** stayed off.
- Defaults: neutral personality, 1,000 prestige, $1,000 (config `StartMoney`).
